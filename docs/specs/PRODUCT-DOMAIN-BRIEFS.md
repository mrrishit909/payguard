# Authoritative product domain briefs

Extracted verbatim from Project Atlas master directive v1.0. This is scope, not research evidence or implementation status. All products are unimplemented in this document. Domain-specific policies must be sourced and versioned before authoritative claims. PayGuard slice acceptance is in PAYGUARD-VERTICAL-SLICE.md.


## PRODUCT 1 — PAYGUARD

### Business problem

Construction specialty subcontractors experience delayed payments, rejected payment applications, complicated retainage, unapproved change orders, and missed legal notice deadlines.

### Buyer

- Subcontractor owner.
- CFO.
- Controller.
- Contract administrator.

### Core product

A construction payment protection and reconciliation platform.

### Minimum functional capabilities

1. Contract intake.
2. Payment application intake.
3. Change-order register.
4. Retainage calculations.
5. Payment requirement checklist.
6. Missing-document detection.
7. Deadline monitoring based on explicitly configured rules.
8. Invoice and payment reconciliation.
9. Exception investigation.
10. Evidence timeline.
11. Actionable collection worklist.
12. Exportable documentation package.

### Core engine

Build deterministic financial reconciliation and contract-condition validation.

### Important restriction

Do not claim that generated lien deadlines are legally authoritative without verified jurisdiction-specific legal rules and expert review.

### Unique design

Visual language:
- Architectural precision.
- Technical drawings.
- Structured financial timelines.
- Construction-document aesthetics.
- Deep ink, ivory, and restrained industrial accents.

The first interactive demonstration should show how a rejected payment application is diagnosed.

### Success test

Given a known fixture containing incomplete payment applications and conflicting change orders, the system identifies the expected discrepancies and correctly calculates verified financial totals.
